About the band

Tiger Coffee is a 3- to 10-piece funk, soul and rock band from Edmonton. They play crowd-moving covers and open-ended jams, built for packed dance floors and long nights.

They scale to fit the room: most nights a tight 3- or 5-piece, sometimes an 8, and for special occasions the full 10 with horns and extra guitar.

Every Sunday they host the River City Revival Sunday Funk Jam in Edmonton. For 3+ years running they've also been a North Country Fair staple, hosting the fair's Sunday open jam.

They've played The Commercial at Blues on Whyte, Dive Bar, Club97 downtown and the Boston Pizza Westmount patio. They also hosted the North Country Fair Afterbender and led the community jam at Heart of the City in Giovanni Caboto Park.

Bars, patios, festivals, community events, private parties: if there's a floor, they'll fill it.
